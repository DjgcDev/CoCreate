Deploying to Vercel
-------------------
Drag this whole folder in, or `vercel` from inside it. No build step,
no framework preset -- it is a static site. Keep every file together:

  index.html          the page
  three.min.js        the 3D engine (self-hosted on purpose)
  cocreate-reel.mp4   the studio video
  testimonials/       your testimonial clips

three.min.js is loaded from here first, then from public CDNs only as a
backup. Deleting it makes the 3D stage depend on a CDN again.
